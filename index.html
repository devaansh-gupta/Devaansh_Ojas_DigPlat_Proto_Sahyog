What is SHAYOG?

Think of SHAYOG as a coordination layer for businesses that need to work together to fulfil a procurement opportunity.

1
Discover an opportunity

A public or enterprise buyer publishes a requirement. SHAYOG helps suppliers understand the opportunity and its eligibility conditions.

2
Understand supplier capability

MSMEs create profiles describing what they make, where they operate, their capacity and the evidence they hold.

3
Find complementary partners

Matching logic surfaces possible partners. The tender's teaming rules and each supplier's independent decision must be checked.

4
Prepare, then execute

SHAYOG coordinates readiness and milestones. The official buyer portal remains the place for submission, evaluation and award.

Important: SHAYOG does not guarantee a tender win, certify suppliers, arrange financing itself, or decide procurement awards.
